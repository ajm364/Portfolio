01 / My direction

Learning by doing.

I was drawn to engineering technology because I wanted more hands-on work. Engineering seemed more theory-focused to me, and I wanted a path where I could apply what I learn.

That practical approach is what interests me about my major.

02 / My motivation

Working on the future.

I see AI as a big part of the future, and I want to be one of the people working on it. My goal is to develop the skills to work on robots and AI systems, helping make them better and safer.

Who's going to work on the robots and AI? I want to be able to say: me.
